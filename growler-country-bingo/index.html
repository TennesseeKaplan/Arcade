Doctor Bingo

Growler Country · Tallahassee, FL

How to Play

Use the arrow keys or joystick to move. Press Space or A to interact.

Serve food. Match each table’s order icon at the kitchen window. Carry up to two plates. Three missed orders end the shift. You can retry that Wednesday.

Run bingo. Call balls at the mic when prompted. Each ball waits for Doc at the mic. When the room lights up, verify the winning card to finish the shift. Clear five Wednesdays to win.

Take a beer break. Low energy slows Doc down. Interact at the Beer Tap to drink and refill the meter. The shift clock pauses while he drinks.
